Store FAQ

Delivery:
Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days.

Returns:
Customers can return products within 30 days of delivery.
Products must be unused and in original condition.

Refunds:
Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Damaged items:
If an item arrives damaged, contact support within 48 hours with photos of the product and package.
