# Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships you will receive an email with a tracking link. You can
also check order status by providing your order number to support.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, to select countries. See the Shipping Policy for details on delivery times
and customs fees.

Q: How do I change my shipping address after placing an order?
A: If the order has not yet shipped, contact support immediately with the new
address. Once shipped, the address cannot be changed.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and select digital wallets.

Q: How do I contact a human agent?
A: If our AI assistant cannot resolve your issue, ask to speak with a human and a
support ticket will be created for you automatically.
